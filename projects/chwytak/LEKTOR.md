## Prompt dla AI, która ma wygenerować / dokończyć taki film (np. Veo 3.1, Seedance, Runway)

```
Vertical 9:16 brand film, 60 seconds, premium high-tech documentary style for a robotics company (DCS Robotics).
Theme: "One movement. Thousands of repetitions. One goal."

Structure:
1. (0–3 s) Dark physics simulation: a white humanoid robot (Unitree G1 type) stands at a wooden table with a black
   speaker basket. Title appears: JEDEN RUCH. TYSIĄCE POWTÓRZEŃ.
2. (3–21 s) Real footage look: a silver humanoid robot in a lab grips the black speaker basket with one gripper,
   lifts it 10 cm, rotates it and sets it back into a grey fixture on a black base; a human hand resets the basket.
   One continuous take, no cuts, handheld phone camera feel, 60 fps smooth. A small stopwatch overlay counts
   "podniesienie → odłożenie" from lift-off to set-down (about 2 s).
3. (21–25 s) Cut to the same movement in the simulation: cyan LiDAR-style scan line sweeps the frame, the scene
   becomes a glowing point cloud for a moment, then solid again. Label: SYMULACJA.
4. (25–44 s) Second real take from another angle, uncut, same movement twice, stopwatch again.
5. (44–48 s) Simulation insert: rotation of the basket, close on the gripper.
6. (48–56 s) Third real take from the table side, uncut.
7. (56–60 s) White end card with the DCS Robotics logo (cyan "DCS", grey "robotics"), text: Uczymy roboty pracy.

Constant small white plate with the DCS Robotics logo in the top-right corner. Captions: large white karaoke
captions at the bottom following the Polish narration word by word. Music: calm pulsing synth, a hit on each cut,
rising tension before each grasp, warm chord on the end card. No invented robot features, no drones, no fake UI data.
```
